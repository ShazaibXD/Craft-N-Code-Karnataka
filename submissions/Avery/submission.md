## Project summary

MINDTRACE finds out why a student got a problem wrong (a careless slip, a unit error, or a missing prerequisite), asks one short question only when the evidence is thin, teaches just the missing concept, and checks it with a fresh problem. It is for students preparing for exams such as JEE, NEET, GATE and first-year engineering.

**1. What made you choose this problem?**

We noticed that most learning tools tell a student "wrong, here is the answer" and move on. A wrong answer can come from a careless slip, a unit mistake or a missing foundation, and each needs a different fix. Treating them all the same wastes time and hides the real cause. We wanted to build something that finds the cause first and keeps the student's trust by being honest about what it knows and what it only estimates.

**2. Who is it for, and how would they use it in a normal week?**

It is for students preparing for exams such as JEE, NEET, GATE and first-year engineering, and for anyone who wants to understand a topic rather than memorise answers. In a normal week a student does a few problems, shows their working, reads the plain-words diagnosis, fixes the one concept that is missing with a short lesson, and then proves it on a fresh problem. The gap map and progress pages show what is solid and what is due for a quick review. Everything runs in the browser with no account, and their data stays on their device.

**3. If you had another month, what would you build next?**

We would calibrate the mastery model on real student data instead of fixed parameters, and test the optional AI paths (building a map from any question and judging open answers) with real classrooms. We would add a teacher view that shows which concepts a whole class is missing, more subjects with expert-reviewed maps, and a verified maths engine for more kinds of answers. We would also add proper accounts and sync so progress can follow a student across devices.
